The brief is above. A few notes on what the synthesis surfaces when viewed as a whole:

The five insights form a chain with a consistent internal logic: the right metric determines when you see collapse → the right mechanism (gradient rank) tells you where to intervene → the timing problem forces you toward anticipatory intervention → anticipatory intervention depends on a probe → probe construction requires a signal that the unprotected model cannot reliably provide. Each turn's answer closed a gap by opening the next one, which is characteristic of a problem that has genuine depth rather than a clean solution.

The open question deliberately points at the one dependency that was never fully resolved: whether the constraint built from a pre-fine-tuning snapshot remains valid as the geometry it was designed to protect evolves under the constrained fine-tuning itself. That's where the next serious theoretical work would need to go.
