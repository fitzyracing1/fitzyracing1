The brief is above. A few notes on the synthesis choices:

The five insights are ordered to track the logical dependency chain: the circularity problem motivates why you need better measurement → measurement reveals structural interference → interference has a temporal window → monitoring that window is harder than expected → the hardest part is that the thing you're monitoring is partly self-generating. Each insight builds the case for why the open question is genuinely open rather than merely unsolved for engineering reasons.

The open question is framed as a control theory problem rather than a measurement problem because, after eight turns, that's where the argument actually lands: the reason no one has solved mixture optimization isn't lack of data or compute alone, it's that the system being optimized is non-stationary and partly self-referential in a way that makes standard offline formulations inadequate.
