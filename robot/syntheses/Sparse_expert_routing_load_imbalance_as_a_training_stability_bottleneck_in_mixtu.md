The brief is above. A few notes on the synthesis choices:

The five insights are ordered to follow the causal chain: why collapse happens (insight 1) → why the standard fix is inadequate (2) → what collapse actually consists of as a system state (3) → what this implies for checkpointing (4) → and what it implies upstream for curriculum (5). This ordering makes the brief readable as a logical argument rather than a list of disconnected findings.

The open question deliberately reframes the entire problem: rather than asking how to prevent collapse within the existing paradigm, it asks whether the paradigm's assumption — that specialization should emerge freely — is justified given the evidence that routing is historically contingent and locks in early. That felt like the sharpest unresolved tension across the full conversation.
