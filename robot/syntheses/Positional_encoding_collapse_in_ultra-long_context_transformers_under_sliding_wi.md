The five insights form a dependency chain, not just a list — relay tokens require subspace disentanglement, which requires normalization awareness, which requires optimizer co-design, which requires feedback-stable monitoring. Fixing any one layer while ignoring the others leaves the overall scheme vulnerable at the gap.

The open question is deliberately unsettling: every proposed fix assumes the separated-subspace solution is an attractor that training will stay near. That assumption has never been verified, and the answer likely depends on scale in non-obvious ways — larger models may have more expressive loss landscapes that find profitable ways to collapse the separation that smaller models never discover.
