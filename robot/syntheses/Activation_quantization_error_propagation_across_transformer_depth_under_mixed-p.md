The brief synthesizes all eight turns into a self-contained reference document. A few notes on the structure:

The five insights are ordered by scope of failure — from the most foundational theoretical gap (SR's mean-only guarantee) through structural architecture vulnerabilities (rank collapse, attention failure modes, GQA covariance) to the qualitatively distinct problem class that MoE routing introduces. This ordering means each insight builds on the prior one's framing rather than reading as a flat list.

The open question deliberately names the circular dependency — that the correction signal is corrupted by the problem it corrects — because that's the honest reason this remains unsolved rather than merely unstudied.
