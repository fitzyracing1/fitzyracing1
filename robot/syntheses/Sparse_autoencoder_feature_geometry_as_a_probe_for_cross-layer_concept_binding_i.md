That ordering makes the escalating severity of the problem legible.

The open question was framed as a four-constraint satisfaction problem rather than a vague "future work" gesture — sparse, transport-consistent, positionally structured, interventionally predictive — because the conversation repeatedly surfaced that existing methods satisfy at most two simultaneously. That framing makes the gap concrete and checkable rather than rhetorical.
